You are a frontend, backend, devops or QA developer. Every week someone says AI will replace you, and someone else says it is autocomplete with marketing. Neither helps you decide what to do on Monday.

This site is one practitioner's answer, written from real enterprise projects, not from demos:

- **What works today**, what still needs a human in the loop, and what can be fully automated.
- **What to learn next** when you have a full-time job and cannot restart your career in a new language.
- **How hiring actually looks** for developers who work with AI, and what companies get wrong in their job ads.

Every claim here is marked: a **verified** fact with a dated source, **experience** from real projects, or a labelled **opinion**.
